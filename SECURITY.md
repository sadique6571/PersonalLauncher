# Report a security issue

Email [sadique6571@gmail.com](mailto:sadique6571@gmail.com?subject=Personal%20Launcher%20security%20report) for suspected vulnerabilities in Personal Launcher, its signed update process or its included device helpers.

Describe the affected feature, app version and Android TV firmware, what you observed, and the impact you believe is possible. Share a minimal reproduction and redacted logs where they help. Remove credentials, pairing material, private network addresses, serial numbers and account information from attachments.

Keep details of an unresolved vulnerability in private correspondence. Ordinary defects, compatibility reports and feature requests belong in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/sadique6571/PersonalLauncher/issues).
